What V21 changed

V21 = V20 + no SHORTS when the previous session fell more than 0.8% and VIX is under 24. After a day like that the market bounced: the next session averaged +0.22% and rose 68% of the time, and our fade shorts were selling into it. Above VIX 24 the effect reverses — the selling continues — so those shorts are kept.

It blocks 27 trades on 4 days in six months. It is insurance, not income.

The assumption that matters most: this projection assumes 21 trading sessions a month. We have been trading 6 to 11. At that rate expect roughly a third of these numbers, no matter how good the filter is. Uptime is now the binding constraint — not the signals, and not the execution.
